# THYNKPDF v0.2.2

Redaction and image tools. Everything works offline and needs no account.

## What's new
- **Redact documents for real.** Redaction deletes what you mark from the file. It doesn't just draw a box over it, so the hidden text can't be copied, searched or recovered.
  - Mark content in any of these ways:
    - drag a box
    - select text
    - click an image, or drag over part of one
    - pick whole pages
    - use **Find & redact** to search for words, a regular expression, or common details such as email addresses, phone numbers, card numbers, SINs/SSNs, postal codes and dates
  - Review every mark in a list before you apply. Delete removes a mark and Ctrl+Z brings it back.
  - Applying asks you to confirm and saves a **new file** by default, so your original stays as it was. You can choose a black box or no fill.
  - **Remove hidden information** also clears metadata (author, title), scripts, attachments and comments. It can also flatten forms and remove invisible OCR text.
- **Images.**
  - **Add image:** place a PNG, JPG, WebP, GIF, BMP or AVIF on a page, move, resize or rotate it, and save it into the PDF.
  - **Open images directly**, or turn several photos or scans into one PDF in the order you choose.
  - **Export pages** as PNG or JPG at 72, 150 or 300 dpi. Several pages download as a zip.
- New Redact, Add image, Images to PDF and Export images tiles on the home screen (#38).
